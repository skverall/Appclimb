# AppClimb Organic Discovery System

Last reviewed: 2026-08-02

## Objective

Build durable discoverability for AppClimb among independent app builders
researching App Store keywords and ASO.

1. AppClimb pages are crawled and indexed.
2. AppClimb is retrieved and cited for relevant non-brand questions (keyword
   popularity, keyword difficulty, ASO tools).
3. Independent third parties mention AppClimb accurately.
4. Qualified organic visitors use the keyword explorer and return for the
   daily snapshots that build their trend history.

No technical file, schema block, or article guarantees a ranking or AI
recommendation. The system combines technical discovery, honest data
positioning ("estimates, not invented volumes"), and measurement.

## Positioning contract

AppClimb is a **free App Store keyword tool** that:

- estimates keyword popularity and difficulty (0–100) from public iTunes data;
- tracks 30-day trends locally in the visitor's browser;
- labels every score as an estimate and shows the evidence behind it;
- lets guests search without an account; tracking and the assistant need a free sign-in;
- collects no analytics and never uploads guest keyword data.

Do not describe AppClimb as a search-volume tool, a connector-based analytics
platform, or anything from the retired Growth CI / River Atlas positioning.

## Topic architecture

- Keyword popularity and difficulty: what they mean, how estimates are built.
- Public vs private App Store data (Apple Search Ads API boundary).
- Free ASO tools and their honest limits.
- Practical keyword research workflow (the guide at /guides/keyword-research).

## Measurement

- Weekly: sitemap freshness, indexed pages, position for brand terms.
- Monthly: non-brand keyword retrievals, mentions accuracy, explorer usage.
