# AppClimb Pricing

Last updated: 2026-08-17

## Guest (no account)

- Price: USD 0
- Card required: No
- Account required: No
- Includes: Keyword Explorer (8 checks/day), official Apple Ads popularity when available (1–100, 30 lookups/day), estimated difficulty (0–100), 30-day trend charts, related keywords, top-app breakdowns, 16 storefronts
- Does not include: tracked apps, ASO assistant
- Data location: your browser only

## Free account

- Price: USD 0
- Card required: No
- Account required: Yes (Google or email magic link)
- Includes: everything in Guest, plus 1 tracked app with 25 keywords and the AI assistant (5 messages/day)
- Data location: your browser only

## Pro

- Price: USD 8/month, or USD 64/year (≈ USD 5.33/month)
- Card required: Yes (via Paddle, merchant of record)
- Account required: Yes
- Includes: unlimited keyword checks, 500 official popularity lookups/day, 90-day trend history, unlimited apps and keywords in My Apps, AI assistant (200 messages/day), cloud sync across devices, cancel anytime

## What you never pay for

- Honest labeling is free: popularity is Apple Ads official (relative 1–100) or a labeled iTunes estimate; difficulty is always estimated; neither is search volume.
