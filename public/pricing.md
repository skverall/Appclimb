# AppClimb Pricing

Last updated: 2026-10-01

## Guest (no account)

- Price: USD 0
- Card required: No
- Account required: No
- Includes: Keyword Explorer (8 new keyword checks/day; re-checks free), Apple Ads popularity (1–100) with 12 weeks of Apple's weekly history, difficulty estimate with evidence, verdict per keyword, trending and related searches from Apple's published list, top-10 app breakdowns, 16 storefronts
- Does not include: tracked apps, ASO assistant
- Data location: your browser only

## Free account

- Price: USD 0
- Card required: No
- Account required: Yes (Google or email magic link)
- Includes: everything in Guest with 30 new keyword checks/day instead of 8, plus 1 tracked app with 25 keywords and the AI assistant (5 messages/day)
- Data location: your browser only

## Pro

- Price: USD 8/month, or USD 64/year (≈ USD 5.33/month)
- Card required: Yes (via Paddle, merchant of record)
- Account required: Yes
- Includes: unlimited keyword checks, 52 weeks of Apple popularity history, unlimited apps and keywords in My Apps, 90 days of rank history, AI assistant (200 messages/day), cloud sync across devices, cancel anytime

## What you never pay for

- Honest labeling is free: popularity is Apple Ads official (relative 1–100); terms below Apple's published top 500 are labeled long tail with their ceiling; difficulty is always estimated and shows its evidence; nothing is search volume.
